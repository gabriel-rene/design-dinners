# Design Dinners

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors are Spanish-speaking designers and creative professionals, primarily arriving on mobile through WhatsApp. Organizers use a small protected CMS to publish events and speakers without editing code.

## Purpose

Help a newcomer understand Design Dinners in under 30 seconds, trust the community through its events and speakers, and join the WhatsApp group. Give organizers a lightweight way to maintain future and past events plus speaker profiles.

## Positioning

Design Dinners is a warm, culturally specific design community organized around shared tables, honest conversation, and practical creative exchange—not a generic professional network or conference brand.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: join the WhatsApp group.
- Secondary CTA: register for the next event.
- Memorable line: “El diseño se sienta a cenar.”
- Proof: the archive of past events, speaker roster, event photography, and recognizable community participation.

## Brand Personality

Warm, candid, energetic, and communal. The voice uses food language with restraint and speaks plainly in Puerto Rican Spanish.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, beige editorial templates, corporate conference styling, decorative card grids, and visual systems that overpower the existing mascot and food-inspired identity.

## Design Principles

- Make joining and registering unmistakably clear.
- Treat past events and speakers as proof of a living community.
- Keep the CMS deliberately small and task-focused.
- Preserve the established identity rather than redesigning it during infrastructure work.
- Make mobile and failure states first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, reduced-motion support, Spanish labels, and clear validation/error recovery.
